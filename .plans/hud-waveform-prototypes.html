Four candidates for the indicator that sits in the HUD's state row. All four are driven by the same synthetic audio envelope below, so silence, a breath, and a burst of speech hit each one at the same moment. Scrub the timeline or point them at your real microphone.

What they share

signalRMS over the last 100 ms of mic audio, the same number the app already computes for the speech gate. No FFT. Smoothed with fast attack and slower release; the responsiveness slider is that ratio.
size64 × 14 pt, centred in the state row. Draws in the label's colour so it reads as part of the text, not a widget stuck on top.
not listeningIn transcribing and cleaning there is no audio. Each variant has a quiet indeterminate motion for those states so the row still says "busy", then freezes on done.
costA 60 fps CALayer redraw of a 64 pt canvas is negligible. It stops when the HUD hides.
